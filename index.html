Projects

CRIW 2026 Team Leader

Research in a Stick (RIS)

A USB stick that runs AI offline. No internet, no cloud, no installation. Built for 4.5 million Kenyan students who cannot afford data. Students plug in the USB, open a browser, and get AI chat, a knowledge library, document analysis, and citation formatting.

Python, Ollama, LangChain, ChromaDB, Kiwix, Llama 3.2, Qwen 2.5
Full-Stack TypeScript

Kabarak Smart Attendance System (KSAS)

QR codes that refresh every 5 seconds. Five layers of security before attendance is recorded. Built with React, TypeScript, and Firebase. Live at ksas-ten.vercel.app.

React, TypeScript, Firebase, Tailwind CSS, Vite
