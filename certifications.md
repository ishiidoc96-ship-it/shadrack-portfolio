## About Anthropic Badges

Anthropic Academy issues **verification links** for each completed course. Format:

```
https://academy.claude.com/verify/[unique-id]
```

These links are shareable and verifiable. Use them on your CV, LinkedIn, and applications instead of screenshots.

To find your verification links:
1. Log in to [academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com)
2. Go to your Student Profile
3. Click on each completed course
4. Copy the verification link
